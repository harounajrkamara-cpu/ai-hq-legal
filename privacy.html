Privacy Policy — AI HQ Uploader

Last updated: 5 October 2026

AI HQ Uploader is a private tool used by a single creator to upload his own videos to his own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data we use

Only the access tokens that the account owner grants through the official login of TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, and basic account information (account ID and username) needed to upload videos to that same account.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable by the owner alone. They are never sold, shared or sent to third parties.

What we do with it

Upload videos made by the account owner, as drafts or posts that the owner approves. We do not read messages, followers or other users' data.

Deleting your data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings (e.g. TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token files.

Contact

Questions: via the contact details on the owner's channel page.
